::: step hook
Dan runs sales at a small software company in Dublin. On Monday morning he writes to Claude:

```prompt
Look at what moved in the pipeline last week, flag the deals that have stalled, and put together 5 slides for the team meeting.
```

Before, Dan would have had to decide where to do this: in a normal chat or in a separate Cowork mode. Not any more.
:::

::: step explain
Chat and Cowork have merged into one Claude: {{data.plans.a6_merge_rollout}}.

You write the task, and Claude works out what it needs: an answer in words, or *tools* such as files, connectors and the browser. If the request is unclear, it'll ask.

Still see a toggle called {{data.ui_paths.a6_cowork_switch}}? Then the new interface hasn't reached you yet.
:::

options:
  - text: "Write the task as it is, with a clear result: “put together 5 slides for the team meeting”"
    correct: true
    why: "You no longer pick a mode. Claude understands that slides need tools. Your job is to say clearly what result you want."
  - text: "First find Cowork mode in the settings and turn it on"
    correct: false
    why: "The new interface has no separate mode and nothing to turn on: any conversation can take on a task like this."
  - text: "Open a separate app for long tasks"
    correct: false
    why: "It all happens in the same Claude: on the web, in the desktop app and on your phone."
  - text: "Split the task into 10 short questions, or Claude won't cope"
    correct: false
    why: "Multi-step work is exactly what Claude can run on its own. No need to chop it into questions; just describe the result clearly."
hint: "Think about who now decides whether a task needs tools: you or Claude?"
:::

::: step explain
A *long task* is work with many steps: find the data, sort through it, build a file. Like asking an assistant: “have a summary ready by the morning”.

It runs *in the cloud*, on Anthropic's servers ({{data.plans.a6_cloud_plans}}). Close your laptop and the work carries on.

Claude can only reach folders on your computer while its desktop app is open.
:::

options:
  - text: "The task will keep going: it runs in the cloud, not on the laptop"
    correct: true
    why: "Long tasks run on Anthropic's servers, so a closed laptop doesn't get in the way."
  - text: "Emma can check progress from her phone"
    correct: true
    why: "The task belongs to the account, not the device. The same conversation opens on the phone."
  - text: "If the task needs a folder on the laptop, Claude can't look inside it while the laptop is closed"
    correct: true
    why: "Connected folders are only available through the open desktop app. The task doesn't stop, but it won't see the files on the laptop."
  - text: "As soon as the laptop is closed, all of Emma's files are copied to the cloud"
    correct: false
    why: "Folders stay on the computer. Claude takes a copy of a specific file only when the task needs it, and only from connected folders."
hint: "Separate the two: where the work itself happens and where your files live."
:::

::: exercise match_pairs
id: "a6.1.e3"

question: "Match each request with what Claude will most likely do."
pairs:
  - left: "“How do you say ‘team meeting’ in Spanish?”"
    right: "Answer straight away, no tools"
    why: "A short question Claude already knows the answer to. Tools would only waste time and limit here."
  - left: "“Put the receipts from the ‘September’ folder into an expenses spreadsheet”"
    right: "Use the connected folder and build a file"
    why: "It needs your files and a finished result, which is multi-step work with tools."
  - left: "“Compare prices on three suppliers' websites”"
    right: "Open the websites in a browser"
    why: "The data is on websites, so Claude will go to the browser. We'll look at how that works in a separate lesson."
  - left: "“Do something with the report”"
    right: "Ask a clarifying question first"
    why: "It's not clear what's wanted: shorten it, translate it, make slides? When a request is unclear, Claude asks rather than guesses."
hint: "For each request, ask yourself: is Claude's own knowledge enough, or does it need your files, websites or more detail?"
:::

::: step explain
Two rules while you get used to it.

**Approvals.** By default Claude asks before it acts. You can loosen this, but don't rush.

**Limit.** Every step uses tokens, so a long task uses more of your limit than a question. Keep an eye on it in {{data.ui_paths.settings_usage}}.

Where it's available: {{data.plans.a6_long_tasks_plans}}.
:::
