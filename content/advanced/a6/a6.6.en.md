::: step hook
Tim in Sligo is on the bus to work. He suddenly remembers: by lunchtime he needs a summary of a month's reviews of the new menu at his café.

His laptop is at home, and the office is 40 minutes away. Can he get started right now, from his phone?
:::

::: step explain
He can: the task belongs to the account, not the device. You set it up in the Claude app on your phone, and it runs in the cloud.

When Claude finishes or needs your answer, you'll get a *notification*. You can carry on in the same conversation on your computer.

Available: {{data.plans.a6_mobile_plans}}.
:::

::: step explain
Where's the best place to look at the result? Artifacts, such as documents and slides, don't open on a phone yet. View them on your computer or on the web.

If the task needs *local* files from your laptop, the browser or computer use, that only works through the Claude desktop app, and only while it's open.
:::

::: step explain
On a phone people write shorter messages, and it's easy to lose what matters. Even on the bus, state the result, the “done” criterion and the boundaries.

If you don't want the task to use your memory or add to it, turn off {{data.ui_paths.chat_memory_toggle}} before sending.
:::

::: step summary
**A task lives in your account, not on a device.** Start on your phone, answer notifications, pick up the result on your computer.

View artifacts on your computer or on the web. Local files, the browser and computer use only work while the Claude app is open on your computer. Even a short prompt from your phone should include the result and the boundaries.
:::
