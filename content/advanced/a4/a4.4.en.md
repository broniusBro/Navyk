::: step hook
Rachel connected everything she could find: Notion, Asana, Linear, Zapier, Stripe and a dozen more services. “Might as well have them to hand.”

Now her long contract reviews get cut off earlier: the conversation hits the length limit far too quickly. It's the same contract. So what's changed?
:::

::: step explain
Every connector *takes up space in the conversation*. Claude's help centre says so directly.

A couple of connectors is barely noticeable. Fifteen, and there's less room left for your document, code and chat history. It's like a backpack: the more “just in case” stuff you pack, the less of what you actually need will fit.
:::

::: step explain
That's what the {{data.ui_paths.a4_tool_access}} setting is for: {{data.ui_paths.chat_plus_menu}} → {{data.ui_paths.a4_chat_connectors}} → {{data.ui_paths.a4_tool_access}}. There are three modes:

- {{data.ui_paths.a4_mode_auto}}: Claude decides what to load for the task;
- {{data.ui_paths.a4_mode_always}}: everything is loaded from the first message;
- {{data.ui_paths.a4_mode_on_demand}}: Claude searches for the right connector based on your prompt and loads only that one.
:::

::: step explain
Two more details.

The mode is chosen *for a specific conversation*. New chat, new choice.

And don't forget about usage limits: tool use counts towards them too. So turn off connectors in a chat that the task doesn't need, and disconnect services you haven't used in ages altogether.
:::

  - text: "Connect the same service a second time"
    correct: false
    why: "A duplicate won't speed anything up; it'll just take up a bit more space."
hint: "Anything that reduces the number of loaded connectors or the length of the history helps."
:::

::: step explain
In {{data.ui_paths.a4_mode_on_demand}} mode, Claude looks for a connector based on what your prompt means. So the more clearly you name the service and the task, the more accurately it finds the right one.

“Have a look at what's there” doesn't tell it where to look. “Search Asana in the ‘Website launch’ project” does.
:::

::: step summary
**Every connector takes up space in the conversation. Lots of connectors: switch to {{data.ui_paths.a4_mode_on_demand}}. A few you always need: {{data.ui_paths.a4_mode_always}}. Not sure: leave it on {{data.ui_paths.a4_mode_auto}}.**

Name the service in your prompt directly, and turn off what you don't need.
