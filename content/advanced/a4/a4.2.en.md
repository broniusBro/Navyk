::: step hook
Ben is a project manager. His team's tasks live in Asana, and he's decided it's time Claude took a look in there.

He opens the settings and finds dozens of services. Where does he start, and how does he avoid clicking something he shouldn't?
:::

::: step explain
Ready-made connectors are collected in the *directory*. It's a curated set of recommended services: Notion, Asana, Linear, Zapier and many more.

The path is: {{data.ui_paths.a4_customize_connectors}} → {{data.ui_paths.a4_add_plus}} → pick a service → {{data.ui_paths.a4_connect}}.

Next, the service's own sign-in opens: you log in to your account and confirm access.
:::

::: exercise order_steps
id: "a4.2.e1"
type: "order_steps"
question: "Put the steps for connecting Asana from the directory in order."
shuffle: true
items:
  - text: "Open {{data.ui_paths.a4_customize_connectors}}"
    why: "This is where all your connectors live: the connected ones and the way into the directory."
  - text: "Click {{data.ui_paths.a4_add_plus}} and find Asana"
    why: "You can browse the directory by category or as a whole, or search for the service you need straight away."
  - text: "Read the description: what the connector can read and what it can write"
    why: "It takes a minute and shows what you're giving access to. Read it before clicking the button, not after."
  - text: "Click {{data.ui_paths.a4_connect}}"
    why: "This button starts the connection."
  - text: "Sign in to your Asana account and confirm access"
    why: "Sign-in happens on the service's side. That's how Claude gets access with your permissions."
hint: "First find, then check, then connect. Signing in to the service comes after you click the button."
:::

::: step explain
Connecting isn't the end of it. You also need to *turn the connector on in the chat* where you need it.

In the message box, click {{data.ui_paths.chat_plus_menu}} → {{data.ui_paths.a4_chat_connectors}} and switch on the toggle next to the service. This applies to that conversation.

That way, your work Asana won't get in the way of a chat about your holiday.
:::

::: step explain
Two caveats.

**Work account on Team or Enterprise.** First, {{data.plans.a4_team_admin}} turns on the connector for the whole organisation. Then each employee signs in to their own account for the service.

**Phone.** Connecting from a phone is only available in beta for now. It's more reliable to do it on the web or in Claude Desktop.
:::
