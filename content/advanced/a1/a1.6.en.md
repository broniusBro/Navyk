::: step hook
Someone sent Conor from Athlone a link in his work group chat: “Brilliant salary and tax calculator, made in Claude! Put in your details and it'll work out your holiday pay.”

Looks handy. But who made it? Where will his salary figures end up? And will anyone else see them?

Two skills today: sharing your own artifacts and opening other people's with care.
:::

::: step explain
Any new artifact is visible **only to you** at first. To share it, open it and click {{data.ui_paths.a1_share}}.

On {{data.plans.a1_visibility_personal_plans}} there are two options: {{data.ui_paths.a1_visibility_only_you}} or {{data.ui_paths.a1_visibility_link}}. On {{data.plans.a1_org_plans}} you also get {{data.ui_paths.a1_visibility_org}}.

The recipient needs a Claude account. You change sharing settings on the website or in the desktop app; you can't do it in the mobile app.
:::

    why: "A link to the latest version will show future edits too. A link to a specific version locks in the one that was approved."

:::

::: step explain
Now for other people's artifacts. The rule from Claude's help centre: **treat someone else's artifact like a file from a stranger.** Only open ones from people you trust.

Before you enter any data, check:

- who the creator is;
- whether there's **shared storage**: anything you enter could be seen by others;
- why it needs your data.

If you're unsure about the source, don't use its code as the basis for your own artifact.
:::

::: exercise multi_choice
id: "a1.6.e3"
type: "multi_choice"
question: "Which signs should make Conor wary of the “salary calculator”? Select all that apply."
options:
  - text: "The link was forwarded “from another chat” and nobody knows who made it"
    correct: true
    why: "An unknown creator is the first reason for caution. The help centre advises opening artifacts only from people you trust."
  - text: "The calculator asks for his card number and an SMS code “to verify your tax”"
    correct: true
    why: "None of that is needed to work out holiday pay. Card numbers and SMS codes never go into anything like this. It's a sign of a scam."
  - text: "On first entry, Claude warns that the data will go into shared storage"
    correct: true
    why: "Claude asks for confirmation the first time an artifact writes to shared storage. Anything that goes there can be seen by other users. His salary definitely shouldn't end up there."
  - text: "The calculator asks for his base salary and number of days off"
    correct: false
    why: "That's exactly the data needed to work out holiday pay. That's fine in itself, as long as the creator is someone he knows and the storage is personal."
hint: "Break it down into three questions: who made it, what it asks for and where that gets saved."
:::

::: step summary
**Your artifacts are private by default, and you choose the access: only you, via link, your organisation, with view, comment or edit rights.**

Treat someone else's artifact like a file from a stranger: check who made it, what it asks for and whether there's shared storage. Never enter passwords, card numbers or codes.

Next up: dashboards from your own data.
:::
