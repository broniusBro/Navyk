::: step hook
Liam from Cork asked Claude to put together a guide for new staff at his café: shifts, recipes, how to run the till.

The answer didn't get lost in the conversation. A separate window opened to the right of the chat with the finished guide: he can edit it, come back to it next week and show it to the manager.

That's an *artifact*. Let's look at when one appears and where to find it later.
:::

::: step explain
An **artifact** is something Claude makes for you that you'll show to other people: a document, a presentation, a mockup, a dashboard, a mini app.

Claude creates an artifact when the result is:

- substantial and self-contained ({{data.limits.a1_artifact_size_hint}});
- something you need outside the chat: to edit, forward or use;
- understandable without the whole conversation;
- something you'll come back to.

A short answer (“How long do I boil a soft-boiled egg?”) stays a message in the chat.
:::

::: exercise single_choice
id: "a1.1.e1"
type: "single_choice"
question: "Which of these requests is Claude most likely to turn into an artifact?"
options:
  - text: "“Draft a one-year lease for a garage, I'll print it out and sign it with my neighbour”"
    correct: true
    why: "A long, standalone text that will be edited and used outside the chat. It ticks every box for an artifact."
  - text: "“What's the Spanish for ‘garage’?”"
    correct: false
    why: "A one-word answer. It doesn't need a separate window, a chat message is enough."
  - text: "“Why did the answer in my chat stop mid-sentence?”"
    correct: false
    why: "That's a question with a short explanation. Nobody will edit it or show it to others, so it's a regular reply."
  - text: "“Thanks, that's clear!”"
    correct: false
    why: "That's just a line in the conversation. Claude will reply in one sentence, there's no output to work with here."
hint: "Ask yourself: would you want to print this result, forward it or tweak it next week?"
:::

    why: "A large, structured document Fiona will keep coming back to all year. A classic artifact."
  - text: "A small “Guess the colour” quiz game to play on a tablet in class"
    correct: true
    why: "Small interactive tools are artifacts too. The game can be opened separately from the chat and played in class."
  - text: "A notice for parents about a new group, which she'll post in the parents' WhatsApp group and pin on the door"
    correct: true
    why: "A finished text that makes sense without the conversation and will go to other people. It makes sense to keep it separate so she can tweak it and print it."
  - text: "The question “How do I get poster paint off a wooden floor?”"
    correct: false
    why: "An everyday question with a short answer. She'll read it once, so it's a regular message."
hint: "An artifact is a result for work and for other people. A “how do I…” question usually gets a short answer in the chat."
:::

::: step explain
All your artifacts are stored in {{data.ui_paths.a1_artifacts_tab}}. You can also start a new one there from a *template*, a ready-made format for the task. You can also pick a template right where you type: {{data.ui_paths.a1_output_menu}}.

- **Docs**: a document;
- **Slides**: a presentation;
- **Design**: a mockup or landing page;
- **Dashboards** (beta; {{data.plans.a1_dashboards_plans}}): charts from your data;
- **Motion** (beta; {{data.plans.a1_motion_plans}}): animation.

Older artifacts created before {{data.plans.a1_legacy_cutoff}} still work, but you can't create new ones of that kind.
:::

::: step explain
There are three ways to change an artifact:

1. Ask Claude in the chat: “Add a section on annual leave.”
2. Edit it yourself: type in the document, move elements around in the mockup.
3. Select some text, click {{data.ui_paths.a1_edit_with_claude}} and describe the change.

Something broken in an artifact? Click {{data.ui_paths.a1_try_fixing}}. It isn't guaranteed to work, sometimes it takes another try.

No artifacts at all? Check {{data.ui_paths.a1_code_execution_toggle}} in {{data.ui_paths.a1_settings_capabilities}}.
:::

  - text: "Select that paragraph, click {{data.ui_paths.a1_edit_with_claude}} and write what to change"
    correct: true
    why: "That way Claude knows exactly which part to touch and won't rewrite the rest. Quick and precise."
  - text: "Ask in the chat: “Rewrite the guide from scratch, but better”"
    correct: false
    why: "“Better” how? Claude might change things Liam was happy with. An edit should be specific."
  - text: "Start a new chat and explain everything about the café again"
    correct: false
    why: "Extra work: the artifact already exists and can be edited in place. A new chat is for a new task, not for fixing one paragraph."
  - text: "Do nothing: an artifact can't be changed once it's created"
    correct: false
    why: "It can. You edit an artifact in the chat, by hand or by selecting text."
hint: "When you need a targeted edit, show Claude exactly which part to change."
:::

::: step summary
**An artifact is a standalone result for real work: a document, presentation, mockup, dashboard or small app.**

It opens next to the chat, is stored in {{data.ui_paths.a1_artifacts_tab}}, and you can edit it with Claude or by hand.

Next up: the first type of artifact, the living document in Docs.
