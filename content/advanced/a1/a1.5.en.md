::: step hook
Laura from Dublin teaches Spanish to adults. Every week her students send her ten sentences each to check, and her evenings disappear into corrections.

She asked Claude to build a practice tool: a student writes a sentence, and Claude checks it right *inside the artifact* and explains the mistake. Laura sent the link to her group. Then she started to wonder: “Is all of this running on my account now?”
:::

::: step explain
An artifact can call Claude on its own. That gives you a *mini app with AI inside*: a practice tool, an idea generator, a helper for filling in applications.

The key points about who pays:

- every user signs in **with their own Claude account**;
- usage comes **out of their limits**, not the creator's;
- no API keys needed, and sharing is free.

A new artifact will ask for permission the first time it wants to call Claude.
:::

::: step explain
A mini app can remember data between sessions (plans: {{data.plans.a1_apps_storage_plans}}). There are two kinds of storage:

- **personal:** each person sees only their own data;
- **shared:** everyone sees the same thing.

The creator decides what data goes where, so spell it out in your prompt. Limit: {{data.limits.a1_storage_max}}.
:::

  - left: "A student's own mistakes and progress in the practice tool"
    right: "Personal storage"
    why: "Other students shouldn't see one student's mistakes. Personal storage keeps data visible only to the person who entered it."
  - left: "A shared list of words of the week that the whole group sees"
    right: "Shared storage"
    why: "The list needs to be the same for everyone, so it belongs in shared storage."
  - left: "A student's phone number and home address"
    right: "Don't store it in the artifact at all"
    why: "The practice tool doesn't need this data. It's best not to collect unnecessary personal data at all."
hint: "Ask yourself: who should see this data: just that person, everyone or no one?"
:::

::: step explain
A good prompt for a mini app reads like a brief for a developer:

1. **What the app does** and who it's for.
2. **What the user enters.**
3. **What Claude does** with that input and what form the answer takes.
4. **What to save** and where: in personal or shared storage.

If something breaks, click {{data.ui_paths.a1_try_fixing}}.
:::

**In a mini app with AI, every user works under their own account and uses their own limits, not the creator's.**

Describe the app like a brief: what it does, what the user enters, what Claude replies, what to save and where. Don't collect personal data you don't need.
