::: step hook
Ciara bakes custom cakes in Kilkenny. She needs a simple page she can send her Instagram customers to.

She asked Claude to “make a nice website” and got a tidy page in lilac tones with the heading “The Best Cakes in Town”. Pretty. But Ciara's brand colours are chocolate and mint, and that's not how she writes at all.
:::

::: step explain
**Claude Design** makes mockups, landing pages, prototypes and one-pagers. It's available {{data.plans.a1_templates_plans}}: ask in the chat or choose {{data.ui_paths.a1_output_design}}.

For a design that's yours and not “average”, give Claude:

- the page's **goal** and **audience**;
- the **layout**: which sections, in what order;
- the **content**: your own text, prices and facts;
- **style references**: screenshots, photos, your logo, an old presentation.
:::

::: exercise find_error

prompt: "Make a landing page for my custom cakes in Kilkenny, so customers from Instagram send an enquiry. Style — well, nice and modern. Make up the text yourself, prices somewhere in the middle."
segments:
  - text: "Make a landing page for my custom cakes in Kilkenny, "
    weak: false
    why: "It's clear what kind of page it is and what business it's for."
  - text: "so customers from Instagram send an enquiry. "
    weak: false
    why: "A great part: there's a goal and an audience. Claude will put the enquiry button somewhere prominent and make the page work well on a phone."
  - text: "Style — well, nice and modern. "
    weak: true
    why: "Everyone's idea of “nice and modern” is different. Better to attach photos of her cakes and her logo, and name the colours: chocolate and mint."
  - text: "Make up the text yourself, prices somewhere in the middle."
    weak: true
    why: "Made-up prices on a page are a promise to customers. Only Ciara knows her own copy and real prices, so she needs to provide them."
hint: "Find the places where Ciara hands Claude things only she knows."
:::

::: exercise multi_choice
id: "a1.4.e2"
type: "multi_choice"
question: "What should Ciara attach to her prompt so Claude Design matches her style? Select all that apply."
options:
  - text: "Photos of her five best cakes"
    correct: true
    why: "Images and screenshots can be added as style references. They help Claude pick up the mood and colours."
  - text: "A screenshot of her Instagram profile"
    correct: true
    why: "That's Ciara's existing style: colours, fonts, presentation. A good reference for a page Instagram customers will land on."
  - text: "Her price list as a document"
    correct: true
    why: "Documents can be attached too. That way the prices on the page will be real, not “somewhere in the middle”."
  - text: "A link to a well-known bakery chain's website, asking for it to be “exactly the same”"
    correct: false
    why: "Copying another brand's identity is a bad idea, both for her brand and legally. Better to describe what she likes: “lots of white space, big photos, order button at the top”."
hint: "Style references are your own materials. Copying someone else's brand wholesale isn't a good idea."
:::

::: step explain
The first version is ready. How to refine it:

- **Chat**: for big changes. “Move the reviews above the prices”, “Make a mobile version”.
- **A comment on the design**: for a single element. “This button is too small.”
- **Editing on the canvas**: drag, resize and align things yourself.

Done? {{data.ui_paths.a1_export}}: {{data.limits.a1_design_export_formats}}. There's no version history yet, so save a copy before any big rework.
:::

::: exercise write_prompt
id: "a1.4.e5"
type: "write_prompt"
question: "Write a prompt for Claude Design: a landing page, one-pager or app screen mockup for a real task of yours. Include the goal, audience, page sections and content, and describe the style references you'll attach. Don't include real phone numbers, addresses or other people's data."
placeholder: "Make a landing page for… Goal… Audience… Sections…"
ideas:
  - "What should visitors do: book, buy, call?"
  - "Who are the visitors, and what device are they on?"
  - "Which sections, in what order?"
  - "What text, prices and facts will you provide?"
  - "Which style references will you attach: photos, logo, screenshots?"
  - "What do you like style-wise: colours, mood, fonts?"
min_chars: 100
hint: "Go through the four points from the help centre: goal, layout, content, audience. Then add style: what you'll attach and what exactly you like."
review:
  task: "An Advanced-level learner is writing a prompt for Claude Design: a landing page, one-pager or app screen mockup for a task of theirs. The help centre advises naming the goal, layout, content and audience; style is set with references (screenshots, photos, documents, presentations) and a description. A strong prompt: a clear page goal and visitor action (R1); the audience and situation, e.g. “they arrive from Instagram on their phones” (R2); sections and their order listed, style described with concrete features and references rather than “nice” (R3); the learner provides real text and prices or asks Claude not to make them up (R4). The learner may just mention that they'll attach references. If they ask to copy another brand exactly, gently suggest describing what they like in their own words."
  criteria: ["R1", "R2", "R3", "R4", "R6"]
  reference_prompt: |
    Make a landing page in Design for my ceramics studio “Clay” in Ghent. The goal is for people to book a taster workshop using a “Book now” button.

    Visitors are people aged 25–40 who come from Instagram on their phones, so mobile first.

    Sections in this order:
    1. Hero: a photo of hands at a pottery wheel, the headline “Make your first cup in 2 hours”, a “Book now” button.
    2. How the workshop works: 3 steps with icons.
    3. Prices: taster session €35, pass for 4 sessions €120.
    4. Reviews: 3 cards, I'll attach the text.
    5. FAQ.
    6. The booking button again.

    Style: I'm attaching our logo, 6 photos from the studio and a screenshot of our Instagram. What I like: warm earthy colours, lots of white space, big photos, a simple font with no flourishes. Take the text from the attached files; if anything is missing, leave a note instead of making it up.
  show_reference: true
  fallback_feedback:
    - "Is it clear what a visitor to the page should do?"
    - "Does it say who the visitors are and what device they use?"
    - "Are the page sections and their order listed?"
    - "Are you providing your own text, prices and facts, or asking Claude not to make them up?"
    - "Is the style described specifically: colours, mood, the references you'll attach?"
    - "Is there no request to copy another brand exactly?"
    - "Is the prompt free of real phone numbers, addresses and other people's personal data?"
