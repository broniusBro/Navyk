::: step hook
Sarah in Brussels manages her department's budget in Google Sheets. She used to download the spreadsheet, upload it to a Claude chat, get a new file back and copy the changes over by hand.

Fifteen minutes of shuffling files every time. Could she just bring Claude into the spreadsheet?
:::

::: step explain
She can. There's a *Claude for Google Workspace* app: Claude opens as a sidebar right inside Google Docs, Sheets and Slides. It sees the open file and edits it in place.

It's like inviting a colleague to sit at your computer instead of emailing the file back and forth.

Availability: {{data.plans.a2_gws_plans}}.
:::

::: step explain
How to get started:

1. Install the {{data.ui_paths.a2_gws_marketplace_app}} app.
2. Open a document and choose {{data.ui_paths.a2_gws_open}}.
3. If the file was open before you installed it, reload the page.

It works in {{data.limits.a2_gws_browsers}}.

On a work account, a Google Workspace admin may block the install — in that case, ask them to allow the app.
:::

::: step explain
At the bottom of the sidebar there's an *editing mode* switch:

- {{data.ui_paths.a2_gws_mode_ask}} — Claude asks before every edit;
- {{data.ui_paths.a2_gws_mode_accept}} — ordinary edits go through without pausing. It still checks with you before adding links, external images or web addresses, or changing sharing.

Everything it's done can be found in {{data.ui_paths.a2_gws_version_history}} and rolled back.
:::

::: step explain
Inside a document, Claude sees the whole file but doesn't know what matters *to you*. So a good task gives an address: a cell, a tab, a section, a slide number. And it explains why, and what the result should look like.

Practise on Sarah's task. Navyk will send your prompt to Claude for feedback.
:::

::: step summary
**Claude in a Google file is a colleague at your computer.** It edits the file in place, and the edits are recorded under your name.

For important documents, stick with {{data.ui_paths.a2_gws_mode_ask}}, give tasks with an address — cell, section, slide — and check the version history.

You've completed the “Documents and spreadsheets” module!
:::
