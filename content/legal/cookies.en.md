Cookies are small files that a website saves in your browser. Your browser's local storage (localStorage) works in a similar way. This page lists everything Navyk stores on your device. The service is operated by {{company.name}}; for details on how we process data, see our [Privacy Policy]({{link.privacy}}).

## 1. Strictly necessary: always on

Without these, signing in and saving your progress would not work. They do not require consent (Art. 399 of the Polish Electronic Communications Law (Prawo komunikacji elektronicznej), Art. 5(3) of EU Directive 2002/58/EC).

- **`__Host-navyk_session`** (cookie): keeps you signed in. Stored for up to 30 days after your last activity, or until you sign out. Not accessible to page scripts.
- **`__Host-navyk_oauth`** (cookie): protects Google sign-in against forgery. Lasts 10 minutes and is used only while you sign in.
- **Local storage `navyk:*`**: your progress, selected language, course cache, a random device identifier for the review limit, and your analytics choice. Kept on your device until you clear it; when you sign out, the progress stored on the device is erased.

## 2. Analytics: only with your consent

If you clicked "Allow" in the analytics banner, Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited) is loaded. It helps us understand which lessons people complete and where learners drop off.

- **`_ga`**: distinguishes browsers; kept for up to 2 years.
- **`_ga_F974J4HTSF`**: stores the visit state; kept for up to 2 years.

If you declined, these cookies are not set and the Google script is not loaded at all.

## 3. How to change your choice

- Click **"Analytics settings"** in the app footer and choose "Allow" or "No, thanks". Withdrawing consent takes effect immediately; any `_ga` cookies already set can be deleted in your browser settings.
- You can delete any cookies and local storage in your browser settings. If you delete the strictly necessary ones, you will be signed out, and any progress made without an account will be lost.
