## 2. What data we process

**If you learn without an account.** Your progress (completed lessons, XP, streak, reviews) is stored only in your browser or on your device. To apply the limit on free prompt reviews, the app creates a random device identifier, which is not linked to your name or email address.

**If you have created an account:**
- your email address and a flag showing it has been verified;
- your password, stored only as a cryptographic hash; we never know the password itself;
- if you sign in with Google, the Google account identifier and email address that Google shares with us (we do not receive your name, photo or contacts);
- your interface language;
- your learning progress: completed lessons, dates, best score, number of attempts, XP, study days and review schedule;
- visit data: when you opened the app and how long you spent in it (start and end of each visit);
- technical session data: sign-in date and last activity.

**Texts you submit for review.** In "prompting practice" exercises, your text is sent for AI review (see section 4). We do not store these texts in our database. Please do not include personal data in them, whether your own or anyone else's.

**Emails.** We only send service emails: address verification and password resets. In our email log we store a hash of the address rather than the address itself, along with the email type and delivery status.

**Technical data.** Your IP address is used to protect against abuse (rate limiting) and may appear in server logs, which are kept for a limited time and used only for security and troubleshooting.

**Analytics, only with your consent.** If you allow analytics, Google Analytics 4 receives information about the app screens you open, your device and your browser. Without your consent, analytics is not loaded at all. Learn more in our [Cookie Policy]({{link.cookies}}).

We do not sell data, we do not show ads, and we do not make automated decisions that have legal effects on you. An AI prompt review is a learning hint, not a decision of that kind.

## 3. Why we process data and on what legal basis

- **Your account, sign-in, progress sync, exercise reviews and service emails**: to provide the service you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, performance of a contract).
- **Security, abuse prevention, limits, backups and anonymised statistics to improve the course (including in the admin panel)**: our legitimate interest in running the service reliably and securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Google Analytics**: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time by clicking "Analytics settings" in the app footer.
- **Compliance with legal obligations** (for example, accounting, if paid features are introduced): Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

## 4. Who we share data with

Only with providers the service cannot run without. Each of them processes data on our behalf under a data processing agreement:

- **Hetzner Online GmbH** (Germany): hosting. The server and database are located in a data centre in the European Union.
- **Anthropic, PBC** (USA): AI review of your prompts via the Claude API. Only the exercise text and your answer are sent, without your email address or name. Under Anthropic's policies, API inputs and outputs are deleted within 30 days and are not used to train models by default.
- **Sendinblue SAS (Brevo)** (France): sending service emails.
- **Google Ireland Limited** (Ireland): Google sign-in, if you choose it, and Google Analytics, if you have given consent.

Transfers to the USA (Anthropic, Google) are safeguarded by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Beyond this, we may disclose data only in response to a lawful request from public authorities.

## 5. How long we keep data

- Account data, progress and visits: for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, this data is removed from the database immediately and from backups within 14 days.
- Sign-in sessions: up to 30 days after your last activity, or until you sign out.
- Verification and password reset links: 24 hours and 1 hour respectively, after which they are deleted.
- Email log: 90 days.
- Prompt texts submitted for review: we do not store them; Anthropic keeps them for up to 30 days.
- Analytics data: according to the Google Analytics settings, no longer than 14 months.
- Data on your device stays there until you delete it or sign out.

## 6. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:
- obtain information about your data and a copy of it (Art. 15);
- have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16);
- delete your account and data, the "right to be forgotten" (Art. 17);
- restrict processing (Art. 18);
- receive your data in a machine-readable format and transfer it to another service (Art. 20);
- object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21);
- withdraw your consent to analytics at any time; this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal.

To exercise a right, email {{company.email}} from the address your account is registered with. We will reply within one month (in complex cases this may be extended by up to two more months, and we will let you know if so).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Poland, this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl). You may also contact the authority in your country of residence.

## 7. Age

Navyk is intended for users aged 16 and over. If you are under 16, you may create an account only with the consent of a parent or guardian.

## 8. Security

Connections to the website are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as a strong hash. Access to the admin panel is limited to a small number of people, based on roles, and every change to access rights is logged. Database backups are made daily and kept for 14 days.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of the page. We will notify you in advance of any significant changes in the app or by email.
