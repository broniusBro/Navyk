::: step hook
Hannah from Lyon wants to ask Claude how to talk gently with her teenage son about his bad grades. The laptop is shared, and her husband sometimes opens her chat history. And she doesn't want it in Claude's memory either.

There's a mode made for conversations like this. Let's see what it does and what it doesn't.
:::

::: step explain
An *incognito chat* is a conversation that doesn't stay in your history and doesn't go into Claude's memory.

It's like chatting with a stranger on a train: you talk, you get off, and they don't remember you.

How to turn it on: start a new chat outside a project and click {{data.ui_paths.incognito_button}}. You'll see a black border around the chat and {{data.ui_paths.incognito_label}} at the top.
:::

::: exercise single_choice
id: "b5.4.e1"
type: "single_choice"
question: "What will happen to Hannah's incognito conversation?"
options:
  - text: "It won't be in her chat history, won't go into memory and won't show up in past chat search"
    correct: true
    why: "Yes, that's the whole point of incognito. Her husband won't see the chat in her history, and Claude won't bring it up in later conversations."
  - text: "It'll vanish instantly without a trace: nothing will be left on the servers"
    correct: false
    why: "Not quite. An incognito chat is kept on the servers for a while, then deleted. It isn't used to train models. You just won't see it again."
  - text: "It'll be saved in her history, but marked with a ghost icon"
    correct: false
    why: "No, it won't be in her history at all. That's why anything important from an incognito chat needs copying before you close it."
hint: "Think of the stranger on the train: what's left after you've talked?"
:::

::: step explain
What to know:

- Closed an incognito chat? You can't open it again. Copy what you need beforehand.
- There's no incognito inside projects.
- It sees your Instructions for Claude, but not your memory.
- It's kept on the servers for {{data.limits.incognito_retention}}, then deleted. It isn't used to train models.

Available on: {{data.plans.incognito_availability}}.
:::

::: exercise multi_choice
id: "b5.4.e2"
type: "multi_choice"
question: "Select all the true statements about incognito chats."
options:
  - text: "You can't open it again once it's closed"
    correct: true
    why: "Right. A closed incognito chat is gone for you for good. If Claude gave Hannah a useful plan for talking to her son, she should copy it into her notes before closing."
  - text: "Your profile instructions still work in it"
    correct: true
    why: "Right. Incognito doesn't use memory, but it does see your profile settings. If your Instructions for Claude say “reply in British English”, that's what you'll get."
  - text: "You can turn it on inside your “Family” project"
    correct: false
    why: "There's no incognito in projects. There, to have a conversation without memory, you turn off Memory in the “+” menu before the first message."
  - text: "You can save it as a regular chat later if you liked it"
    correct: false
    why: "No, you can't turn an incognito chat into a regular one. If you want the conversation in your history, pick a regular chat from the start."
  - text: "In incognito it's fine to paste your passport number: no one will see it anyway"
    correct: false
    why: "Incognito doesn't make passwords and documents safe: the chat is still kept for a while. The rule stays the same: ID numbers, passwords and card details don't go in any chat."
hint: "Think about three things: what happens after closing, where incognito works, and what it can see from your settings."
:::

::: step explain
From the last lesson you know another option: a regular chat with {{data.ui_paths.chat_memory_toggle}} turned off.

- *Incognito*: not in your history and not in memory. Like chatting with a stranger on a train.
- *Without memory*: stays in your history, but doesn't use or add to memory. Like rough notes in a notebook that you don't show your assistant.
:::

::: step summary
**Don't want it in your history or memory? Use incognito. Want it in your history but without memory? Turn off Memory in “+”.**

Copy anything important from incognito before closing it. Don't put documents or passwords in any chat.
:::
