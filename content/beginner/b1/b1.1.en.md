::: step hook
Hi! Let's dive right in. Here's an everyday situation and a single message to Claude. See what happens.

```prompt
My neighbour Sarah has asked me for the third time to look after her dog Bonnie at the weekend. I'm away this weekend. Help me write her a polite no by text, 2–3 sentences.
```

```claude
Hi Sarah! I'm away this weekend, so I'm afraid I can't look after Bonnie this time. I hope you find someone nearby soon. Have a lovely weekend, both of you!
```

One message, and an awkward text is done. In 4 minutes you'll write your own prompt about a real task of yours.
:::

::: step explain
*Claude* is an AI assistant made by Anthropic.

An *AI assistant* is a program you chat with like a very well-read friend: you write in plain words, and it replies in text.

Your message is called a *prompt*. Think of it like a message to a plumber: “The kitchen tap is dripping. When can you come round?”
:::

    correct: false
    why: "A search engine sends you off to websites, while Claude answers itself, in its own words, and you can keep talking to it. Remember the example: it didn't give you links on “how to say no to a neighbour”, it just wrote the message."

    correct: false
    why: "There's no one on the other end. Claude is a program, so it answers in seconds, at any hour, and never gets tired of questions."
  - text: "A program that knows everything and never makes mistakes"
    correct: false
    why: "Claude knows a great deal, but it sometimes gets things wrong. Even Anthropic, the company that built it, recommends double-checking anything important. More on that later in this lesson."
hint: "Think back to the neighbour example. What did Claude actually do: give you links or write the reply itself?"
:::

::: step explain
Claude can help with anything you do with words:

- write an email or a message;
- explain something complex in simple terms;
- summarise a long text;
- come up with ideas;
- translate;
- make sense of a photo or a chart.

It can make mistakes, so double-check anything important (money, health, documents).
:::

    correct: true
    why: "Explaining confusing things simply is one of Claude's strengths. Check the actual amounts and rates against the bill itself, though: don't trust it to do your money maths for you."
  - text: "Come up with 10 ideas to keep the kids busy on a rainy weekend"
    correct: true
    why: "Claude is good at coming up with ideas. Add the kids' ages and what you have at home, and the ideas get much more useful."
  - text: "Translate a letter from your landlord written in German"
    correct: true
    why: "Translation is a classic Claude task. You can also ask it to explain what the letter wants from you and how to reply."
  - text: "Go to the bank for you and sign a contract"
    correct: false
    why: "Claude works in a chat: it writes, explains and breaks down texts. It can't go anywhere or sign anything for you. But it can explain a contract before you sign it."
  - text: "Remember your online banking password so you don't forget it"
    correct: false
    why: "Never send passwords to any AI chat: it's like reading out your PIN in a busy café. Use a password manager for that."
hint: "Think about which tasks are done with words and text, and which need hands or secret data."
:::

::: step explain
Write to Claude the way you'd write to a smart friend who doesn't know your situation. You need two things: *what to do* and *a couple of details*. Compare:

```prompt
Help with an email
```

```prompt
Help me write an email to my son's class teacher: he'll miss school on Friday because of a doctor's appointment. Short and polite.
```

Claude will get the second prompt right first time.
:::

id: "b1.1.e3"
type: "single_choice"
question: "Maya wants to send her mum a birthday message on WhatsApp for a big birthday. Which prompt will get her a good text fastest?"
options:
  - text: "“Write a warm 60th birthday message for my mum to send on WhatsApp, 3–4 sentences. She adores her garden and her grandchildren.”"
    correct: true
    why: "It has everything: what to do (a birthday message), who and what for (mum, turning 60), where and how long (WhatsApp, 3–4 sentences) and personal details (garden, grandchildren). The text will be about her mum, not “mums in general”."
  - text: "“Birthday message”"
    correct: false
    why: "Two words. Claude doesn't know who it's for, what the occasion is or where it's going. Most likely it'll write a generic template or start asking questions, and you'll have to write again."
  - text: "“Can you write birthday messages?”"
    correct: false
    why: "Claude will say “yes” and ask what you need. That's an extra round of messages. You can just ask for the thing straight away."
  - text: "“Write the best birthday message EVER!!!”"
    correct: false
    why: "The enthusiasm is clear, but there are no details: who it's for, what age, where it's going. “The best” is a mystery to Claude if it knows nothing about the mum."
hint: "Look for the option with both “what to do” and a couple of details about the mum."
:::
