::: step hook
Sophie spent the whole morning working through her coursework materials with Claude. Suddenly, instead of an answer, she got a message: limit reached.

Nothing's broken. That's how *limits* work. Like a mobile data plan: when your data runs out, you wait for it to renew or get a bigger plan.

Let's look at the plans and how to keep an eye on your limit.
:::

::: step explain
Claude has a free plan, **Free**, and paid ones. The first paid plan is **Pro**, and above it is **Max**.

On Free you can chat in the browser, on desktop and on your phone, and search the web. Pro gives you at least 5 times more usage. It also adds the Opus model, in-depth research (Research), Claude Code and as many projects as you like.

Current prices: **claude.com/pricing**.
:::

::: step explain
The *session limit* resets every 5 hours. Like the office coffee machine: by lunchtime it's been refilled.

The *weekly limit* applies on paid plans and resets once a week. It's an overall ceiling, like your data allowance.

On a paid plan you can see your usage in **Settings → Usage**: the **Current session** and **Weekly limits** bars.
:::

::: step explain
What eats into your limit faster: long chats, heavy models, high Effort, web search when you don't need it.

How to make it last:

- new task, new chat;
- for simple things, a lighter model and the default Effort;
- limit reached? Wait for the reset or consider a paid plan.
:::

::: step summary
**Free costs nothing and has a smaller limit. Pro gives more usage, Opus, Research and Claude Code.**

The session limit resets every 5 hours, the weekly one once a week. Keep an eye on both in Settings → Usage. New task, new chat.
