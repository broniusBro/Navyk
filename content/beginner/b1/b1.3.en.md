::: step hook
Liam from Ghent spends the day at his work laptop. In the evening, on the bus, he remembers he wanted to ask Claude about his holiday.

He doesn't have to wait until he's back at the laptop. Claude is on his phone too, and yesterday's conversation is right there.

In 4 minutes we'll cover where to open Claude and which countries it works in.
:::

::: step explain
Claude has three “doors”:

- the *web version*: the claude.ai website in any browser, nothing to install;
- the *desktop app* for Mac and Windows: opens from your desktop, like a messaging app;
- the *mobile app* for iPhone and Android.

It's one account. Like email: you see your messages on both your laptop and your phone. Chats sync the same way.
:::

::: step explain
Claude isn't available in every country. Anthropic keeps an official list: **anthropic.com/supported-countries**. If a country isn't on it, it isn't supported.

Right now the list includes Kazakhstan, EU countries and Ukraine, except for Crimea and the Donetsk, Kherson, Luhansk and Zaporizhzhia regions. Russia and Belarus aren't on the list. The list changes, so check it yourself.
:::

::: step explain
What matters is where you *physically are*, not where your account was created. Like roaming: same phone, but in some countries there's no signal.

If you're currently in an unsupported country, you can't use Claude from there, and you shouldn't try to get around that rule. You can learn with Navyk anywhere.
:::
