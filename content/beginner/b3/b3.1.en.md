::: step hook
Claire in Cork has received new terms from her broadband provider: a six-page PDF in small print. Rather than retyping it, she simply attached the file to her message.

```prompt
[attached file: new_terms.pdf]
These are the new terms from my broadband provider. What's changing for me from November, and do I need to do anything? Answer as a list, 3–5 points.
```

```claude
What changes from 1 November:
- The monthly fee for the “Home 100” plan goes up from €35 to €42 (section 2).
- Your speed stays the same, 100 Mbps.
- The provider's router is now charged separately, €4 a month (clause 3.4).
- You don't need to do anything: the new terms apply automatically. If you don't agree, under clause 5.1 you can cancel the contract with 14 days' notice.
```

Six pages, and the answer took thirty seconds. Let's see how it's done.
:::

::: step explain
To *upload a file* means to attach it to your message, like a photo to an email. Claude reads the file together with your question.

It works with documents (PDF, DOCX, spreadsheets, text) and images (photos, screenshots). Full list of formats: {{data.limits.file_types}}.
:::

::: step explain
There are three ways to attach a file:

1. Click {{data.ui_paths.attach_button}} in the input field and choose {{data.ui_paths.add_files}}.
2. Drag the file with your mouse straight into the chat window.
3. Copy an image or screenshot and paste it into the input field (Ctrl+V).

Then type your question and press {{data.ui_paths.send}}.
:::

    why: "This is the first step of uploading. Instead of the button, you can drag the file into the chat window: same result."
  - text: "Check that the file is attached to the message"
    why: "A quick check that Claude will get the file. Otherwise it'll answer “in general”, without seeing the contract."
  - text: "Type a question about the file"
    why: "A file with no question says nothing about your goal. The question tells Claude what exactly to look for in the contract."
  - text: "Send the message"
    why: "The file and the question go together, so Claude answers with the document in mind."
hint: "Sending always comes last. By the time you type your question, the file should already be attached."

::: step explain
A file with no question is like handing papers to a lawyer and walking out without a word. Say what you need from the document. Compare:

```prompt
Have a look
```

```prompt
This is my son's school timetable for the spring term. On which days does he have 7 lessons and finish after 14:30?
```

In the second case, Claude knows what to look for.
:::

  - text: "“This is my October bill. Why is heating €40 more than in September? Explain it using the lines on the bill.”"
    correct: true
    why: "It's clear what the document is, what exactly is puzzling and what kind of answer is needed. Claude will compare the lines and explain the difference instead of retelling the whole bill."
  - text: "“What's this?”"
    correct: false
    why: "Claude will describe the bill: list the charges and amounts. Sophie can already see that. Her real question about heating goes unanswered."
  - text: "“Check it”"
    correct: false
    why: "Check what: the amounts, the rates, the meter readings? Claude will have to guess, and it won't necessarily guess what you need."
  - text: "“Can you read bills?”"
    correct: false
    why: "An extra round of messages: Claude will say “yes” and ask how it can help. Better to ask the real question straight away."
hint: "Remember the formula from the module on prompts: what to do + a couple of details. It works with files too."
:::

::: step explain
Claude receives the whole file, with everything in it. Before uploading, look through it: are there other people's passport details, card numbers, phone numbers or addresses?

If your question doesn't need them, black them out or upload only the pages you need. Don't send other people's personal data unless you have to.
:::

    correct: true

  - text: "A screenshot of the class parents' group chat with phone numbers, to draft a reply to the teacher"
    correct: true
    why: "Claude doesn't need the other parents' numbers. Crop the screenshot to the messages you need or black out the numbers."
  - text: "A photo of a café menu, to work out how much dinner for four will cost"
    correct: false
    why: "A menu has nobody's personal data, so you can upload it as it is."
  - text: "A school timetable with no surnames, to plan your week"
    correct: false
    why: "Subjects and lesson times aren't personal data. You can send a file like that without worrying."
hint: "Ask yourself about each file: does it contain other people's data, and does my question need it?"
:::

::: step summary
**Attach the file and ask a specific question about it.**

Ways to attach: the add-file button, drag and drop, or pasting an image. Before uploading, remove other people's personal data that your question doesn't need.
