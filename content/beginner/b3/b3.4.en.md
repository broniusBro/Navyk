::: step hook
Andrew in Lisbon is paying his broadband bill on his provider's website, but the payment won't go through. He takes a screenshot and writes:

```prompt
[screenshot attached]
What's this?
```

```claude
The screenshot shows a payment page with an error message: “Transação recusada. Código 05” (in English: “Transaction declined. Code 05”). This message means the payment was not processed.
```

Claude described it all correctly, but Andrew could already see that. He needed something else.
:::

::: step explain
Claude can “look” at images: read the text in a screenshot, interpret a chart, explain what's in a photo. But an image shows *what*, not *why*.

So, just like with a file, an image needs a question with a goal: what you were trying to do, what went wrong and what kind of answer you need.
:::

::: exercise compare_answers
id: "b3.4.e1"
type: "compare_answers"
variant: "which_better"
question: "Andrew sent the same screenshot with a different question. Which answer is more useful to him?"
cards:
  - id: "A"
    label: "Question: “What's this?”"
    prompt: "What's this?"
    answer: |
      The screenshot shows a payment page with an error message: “Transação recusada. Código 05” (in English: “Transaction declined. Code 05”). This message means the payment was not processed.
  - id: "B"
    label: "Question with a goal and context"
    prompt: "I'm trying to pay my home broadband bill with my Irish bank card on the provider's website. Yesterday a payment from this card went through fine. What does this error mean, and what should I do, step by step, to pay today?"
    answer: |
      “Code 05” usually means your bank declined the transaction without giving a reason. Try these in order:
      1. Check your banking app to see whether there's a limit on online payments or a notice that the card has been blocked.
      2. If everything looks fine in the app, call your bank and quote code 05.
      3. While you sort it out, pay another way: by bank transfer to the provider's account details from your banking app, or with a different card.
options:
  - text: "Answer B: Claude knows the goal and the situation, and gives an action plan"
    correct: true
    why: "Yes. Andrew explained what he was doing, on which website, and that it worked yesterday. Claude could explain the error and give steps instead of describing the screen."
  - text: "Answer A: it's shorter and describes the screenshot accurately"
    correct: false
    why: "The description is accurate but useless: Andrew could already see the error. He needed to know what to do next."
  - text: "Both answers are equally useful: it's the same screenshot"
    correct: false
    why: "The image is the same, but the questions are different. Without a goal, Claude can only describe what it sees."
hint: "Think about which answer will help Andrew pay his bill today."
:::

::: step explain
The second condition is *image quality*. Claude's help centre recommends images of at least 1000×1000 pixels. With small, blurry or rotated images Claude makes more mistakes, just like you would trying to read a receipt through frosted glass.

Take a screenshot rather than photographing your screen, and crop out anything you don't need. Maximum image size: {{data.limits.image_max_px}}.
:::

::: exercise multi_choice
id: "b3.4.e2"
type: "multi_choice"
question: "You're photographing your gas bill to ask about the charges. What will help Claude read it correctly? Select all."
options:
  - text: "Shoot in good light, without glare or shadows"
    correct: true
    why: "Glare on the numbers turns the amount into a guess. Even lighting makes a big difference to accuracy."
  - text: "Hold your phone level above the page so the text isn't at an angle"
    correct: true
    why: "Claude reads rotated and skewed images less well. A straight shot is an easy way to avoid that."
  - text: "Capture the whole bill up close so it fills the frame"
    correct: true
    why: "The more pixels the text takes up, the sharper the numbers. The help centre recommends images of at least 1000×1000 pixels."
  - text: "Send a tiny thumbnail from a messaging app, since it uploads faster"
    correct: false
    why: "Messaging apps often compress photos. In a small image the numbers blur together and Claude may misread them. Send the original."
  - text: "Photograph the bill on your monitor instead of taking a screenshot"
    correct: false
    why: "Photographing a screen causes moiré patterns, glare and skew. If the bill is on screen, take a screenshot: it's sharp and straight."
hint: "Think about what makes it hard for you to read small print in a photo."
:::

::: step explain
A few more things to keep in mind:

- Claude may misread numbers in an image. Check important amounts against the original.
- Its object counts are approximate.
- It doesn't identify people from photos.
- A photo of a mole or an MRI scan is a reason to see a doctor. Claude can explain the terms, but it doesn't diagnose.
:::

::: exercise find_error
id: "b3.4.e3"
type: "find_error"
question: "Grace in Leeds is sending a photo of her child's school timetable from the parents' group chat. Tap every weak spot in her message."
prompt: "The photo's tiny, from the group chat, but you'll figure it out. Here's the timetable. Do something with it."
segments:
  - text: "The photo's tiny, from the group chat, but you'll figure it out. "
    weak: true
    why: "A small, compressed image is a common cause of mistakes: Claude might mix up a time or a subject. Better to ask the chat for the original or take a clear screenshot."
  - text: "Here's the timetable. "
    weak: false
    why: "Good: Claude knows it's looking at a timetable and not, say, a menu."
  - text: "Do something with it."
    weak: true
    why: "The goal is unclear. Turn it into a table? Find the PE days? Plan who picks the child up when? Better to say it outright: “Make a table by day and mark the days when lessons finish after 14:00.”"
hint: "Check two things: can the image be seen clearly, and is it clear what to do with it?"
:::

::: exercise single_choice
id: "b3.4.e4"
type: "single_choice"
question: "Mark in Berlin has received a letter in German from the Jobcenter and taken a photo of it. Which message to send with the photo is better?"
options:
  - text: "“This is a letter from the Jobcenter. My German is about A2. Translate it into English and, separately, tell me: what they want from me, by when, and which documents I need”"
    correct: true
    why: "There's a goal (understand what to do), context (A2, Jobcenter) and a format (translation plus a short summary). Claude will answer exactly what Mark needs."
  - text: "“Translate”"
    correct: false
    why: "Claude will translate it, but Mark may miss the key points: the deadline and what to do. The goal, “understand what to do”, isn't stated."
  - text: "“This is important!!! Help urgently!!!”"
    correct: false
    why: "The emotion is understandable, but Claude doesn't know whether Mark needs a translation, advice or a reply to the letter."
  - text: "Send the photo with no text: Claude will work it out"
    correct: false
    why: "Most likely it'll just describe or translate the letter. What Mark actually needs, “what do I have to do and by when”, may get lost."
hint: "Look for the option that says who you are, what you need and in what form."
:::

::: step explain
Now it's your turn. Imagine you've hit an error on a website: a form won't submit, the payment page won't load, you can't log in to your account. You've taken a screenshot.

Write the message you'll send with the screenshot. Black out any logins, passwords and card numbers in the screenshot.
:::

::: exercise write_prompt
id: "b3.4.e5"
type: "write_prompt"
question: "Write a message to go with a screenshot of a website error so that Claude can help you fix it."
placeholder: "[screenshot] I'm trying to… What…"
ideas:
  - "Which website, and what you were trying to do"
  - "What you've already tried"
  - "Device and browser"
  - "What answer you need: an explanation or steps"
min_chars: 40
hint: "Say what you were doing when the error appeared, what you've already tried and what answer you need: for example, steps in order."
review:
  task: "The learner is writing the text they'll send with a screenshot of a website error. The screenshot itself isn't provided: assess only the text. The lesson teaches that an image needs a goal (fix the problem) and context (which website, what they were doing, what they've tried, device or browser), plus the kind of answer they want. If the learner mentions blacking out personal data, that's a plus. If they included a login, password or card number, that's a privacy violation."
  criteria: ["R1", "R2", "R3", "R4"]
  reference_prompt: |
    [screenshot]
    I'm trying to pay my water bill in my online account on the water company's website. After I click “Pay”, I get the error in the screenshot. I've tried refreshing the page and logging in again, but it didn't help. Windows computer, Chrome browser. Explain in plain English what this error means, and give me 3–5 steps to try, in order. If the cause isn't clear from the screenshot, tell me what else to check.
  show_reference: true
  fallback_feedback:
    - "Is it clear which website it is and what you were trying to do?"
    - "Does it say what you've already tried, so Claude doesn't suggest it again?"
    - "Is the device or browser mentioned?"
    - "Is it clear what answer you need: an explanation, steps in order, a message for support?"
    - "Have logins, passwords and card numbers been blacked out in the screenshot?"
:::
