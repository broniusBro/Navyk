::: step hook
Sam runs an online tea shop in Ghent and asks Claude for help answering customers every day. And every time, he starts like this:

```prompt
I sell tea through my online shop, The Tea Shelf. Here's our price list… Here's our delivery policy… Keep replies polite, short and friendly. Now help me answer a customer: she's asking when her order will arrive.
```

Five minutes on the same intro every time. Do you think there's a way to explain it all just once?
:::

::: step explain
There is. That's what a *project* is for ({{data.ui_paths.projects}} in the interface).

A project is a separate workspace for one topic. Picture a “Renovation” folder on your desk: receipts, the kitchen plan and your notes, and you take it to every meeting with the builder.

A project has its own chats, its own files and its own instructions.
:::

::: step explain
Here's the key difference from a regular chat.

A regular chat starts from a blank page: Claude only knows what you've written in that conversation.

A new chat inside a project can see the *project knowledge* (files) and the *project instructions* (how to work) straight away. Sam no longer has to paste in his price list: he added it to the project once.
:::

::: step explain
How to create a project:

1. Open {{data.ui_paths.projects}} in the sidebar (or go to claude.ai/projects).
2. Click {{data.ui_paths.new_project}}.
3. Enter a name and description. These are labels for you: Claude can't see them.

Projects are available on every plan, including Free. On Free you can create up to {{data.plans.free_projects_max}} projects.
:::

::: step summary
**A project is a workspace for one recurring topic: its own chats, plus shared files and instructions.**

Explain once, use it in every new chat. The project name is for you, not for Claude.

Next up: which files to put in the project knowledge.
:::
