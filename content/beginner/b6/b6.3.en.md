::: step hook
Under every Claude response there are “thumbs up” and “thumbs down” buttons. And at the top of the chat there's a button to share the conversation.

They're easy to press. But each of these buttons sends the conversation onwards, beyond your own history. Let's look at where exactly it goes, and then you can press them with peace of mind.
:::

::: step explain
*Rating a response* ({{data.ui_paths.feedback_thumbs}}) is like rating a taxi ride: you help the service get better.

What happens when you press it:

- the **whole conversation** is saved, not just one response;
- it's stored for up to 5 years;
- before it's used, it's disconnected from your account;
- it may be used for research and model training.
:::

  - text: "The whole conversation, including the statement, for up to 5 years"

    correct: false

  - text: "Nothing: a rating is just an icon on the screen"
    correct: false
    why: "A rating is real feedback that goes to Anthropic together with the conversation. The icon is just the start."
  - text: "Only the fact itself: “thumbs down”, without any text"
    correct: false
    why: "A bare thumbs down doesn't explain much. That's why the whole conversation is saved along with the rating."
hint: "Think back to the previous screen: is one response saved, or more?"
:::

    correct: true
    why: "Rating is optional, and the service works perfectly well without it. And if you write “my daughter, 9” instead of her name from the start, you can rate any response without worrying."
  - text: "Give it a thumbs up: a positive rating doesn't save anything"
    correct: false
    why: "Both thumbs work the same way: up and down both save the whole conversation for up to 5 years."
  - text: "Rate it, then delete the chat, and the feedback will be deleted too"
    correct: false
    why: "Under Anthropic's rules, feedback has its own retention period of 5 years, separate from your chat history. It's safer not to send feedback with personal data than to count on deleting it later."
  - text: "Type “thank you” in the chat: that's feedback too, and it goes to Anthropic"
    correct: false
    why: "A “thank you” message in the chat is part of the conversation, not feedback via the button. Only the buttons under a response send a rating."
hint: "Which option doesn't send the conversation with the child's details any further, and helps in the future?"
:::

::: step explain
A *public link* ({{data.ui_paths.share}}) is like a photo of a notebook page: you send a snapshot, not the notebook itself.

- The link shows messages up to the moment you shared it, plus artifacts.
- **Anyone** with the link can open it.
- New messages stay private.
- Attached files aren't included in the snapshot.
:::

::: step explain
Changed your mind? You can turn off access at any time, in two ways:

- in the chat itself: open {{data.ui_paths.share}} and switch Public to Private;
- all links at once: {{data.ui_paths.shared_chats_manage}}, then Unshare next to the chat you want.

After that, the link stops working.
:::

::: step summary
**Ratings and links send your conversation beyond your own history.**

A rating saves the whole conversation for up to 5 years. Anyone with a link can open it until you turn off access. Before you press, check there's nothing personal in the chat.
