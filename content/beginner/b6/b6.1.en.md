::: step hook
Emma from Bristol got her blood test results back and asked Claude to help her make sense of them.

```prompt
Explain in plain words what “haemoglobin 112” and “ferritin 9” mean in my blood test. I'm 34. I'm seeing my doctor on Friday and want to know what to ask.
```

```claude
Haemoglobin carries oxygen, and ferritin shows how much iron your body has stored. Both values are lower than is typical for an adult woman. That's a common reason to talk to your doctor about iron deficiency. You could ask whether you need any further tests and which treatment would suit you.
```

Then Emma wondered: “Will this conversation be used to train Claude?” Let's look at it calmly.
:::

::: step explain
*Model training* is when new versions of Claude learn from large amounts of text. Think of a chef improving recipes based on guests' feedback.

On the Free, Pro and Max plans, your chats are used for training in three cases:

- you've allowed it in your settings;
- the chat has been flagged for a safety review;
- you've separately agreed to take part in testing.
:::

::: step explain
The setting lives here: {{data.ui_paths.settings_privacy}}. Inside, look for the {{data.ui_paths.model_improvement}} toggle.

The path is the same on the website, in the desktop app and in the mobile app: click your name, open the settings, then the privacy section.

Take a look after this lesson and see which position your toggle is in.
:::

::: step explain
What the toggle changes:

- **On.** New and resumed chats may be used for training. There they're stored in de-identified form, not linked to your name, for up to 5 years.
- **Off.** Neither previous nor new chats will be used for future training. But anything already in training that's under way isn't taken back out.
:::

::: step explain
There's no right position for the toggle. On helps improve the models; off gives you more privacy. It's your choice, and you can change it.

Whatever you choose, this works: use an incognito chat for especially personal topics, and delete anything you don't need from your history.

Work accounts for organisations and the API have their own rules. If you use Claude at work, check with your admin.
:::

::: step summary
**Whether Claude learns from your chats is decided by a toggle in {{data.ui_paths.settings_privacy}}, and you're the one in control.**

Turning it off applies to future training. For anything especially personal, there's incognito and deleting the chat.
